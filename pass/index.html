About auto-fill

This web version uses tap-to-copy quick fill: tap Open & Fill on a login and it opens the site, copies your username, then your password — and wipes the clipboard after 30 seconds. No web page can type passwords into other apps or browsers; that is a rule of phones and browsers, which is why the big password apps do it through a phone app. Full automatic save & fill inside other apps and your browser comes with the Mighty Thorn Pass phone app, which is the next step.
